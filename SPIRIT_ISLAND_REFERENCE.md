# Spirit Island Base Game Reference

This document contains verified reference data for the Spirit Island base game (no expansions).

---

## 1. Game Overview

**Spirit Island** is a cooperative strategy board game designed by R. Eric Reuss, published by Greater Than Games. Players take on the roles of powerful island Spirits defending their home from colonizing Invaders. The native islanders, the Dahan, also fight back against the Invaders.

- **Players:** 1-4 (base game)
- **Cooperative:** Fully cooperative; all players win or lose together
- **Play time:** 90-120 minutes
- **Table talk is encouraged:** Players confer freely about strategy

---

## 2. Core Mechanics / Sequence of Play

Each round consists of five phases in order:

### Phase 1: Spirit Phase (All players simultaneously)

Each Spirit performs three steps in order:

1. **Growth:** Choose one Growth option from the Spirit Panel (unless stated otherwise). Growth options typically include combinations of:
   - Reclaim all discarded Power Cards
   - Gain a new Power Card (Minor or Major)
   - Add Presence to the island
   - Gain Energy
   - Complete all parts of the chosen growth option (order is your choice)

2. **Gain Energy:** Receive Energy equal to the highest uncovered number on the Energy Presence Track.

3. **Play and Pay for Power Cards:** Select which Power Cards to use this turn (up to the highest uncovered number on the Card Plays track). Pay the Energy cost of all selected cards immediately. Gain all Elements shown on those cards. Effects resolve later (Fast or Slow phase).

### Phase 2: Fast Power Phase

Resolve all Fast Powers (from Power Cards and Innate Powers) simultaneously. Players choose the order of their own powers.

### Phase 3: Invader Phase

Executed in this order:

1. **Blighted Island Effect:** If the Blight Card is on its "Blighted Island" side, follow its instructions.
2. **Fear Card Resolution:** Flip earned Fear Cards face-up and resolve their effects based on the current Terror Level, then discard.
3. **Ravage:** Invaders deal damage in the land type shown on the Ravage action space card.
4. **Build:** Invaders add buildings in the land type shown on the Build action space card.
5. **Explore:** Reveal the top Invader Deck card; Invaders explore in that land type.
6. **Advance Invader Cards:** Ravage card is discarded, Build card slides to Ravage, Explore card slides to Build.

### Phase 4: Slow Power Phase

Resolve all Slow Powers (from Power Cards and Innate Powers) simultaneously.

### Phase 5: Time Passes

- All played Power Cards go to personal discard piles
- All damage markers, element markers, and single-turn effect tokens are removed

## 3. Victory and Loss Conditions

### Victory Conditions (by Terror Level)

The game starts at **Terror Level 1**. As Spirits generate Fear and fill the Fear Pool, Fear Cards are earned. When a Terror Level divider is revealed, the Terror Level advances and the victory condition changes immediately.

| Terror Level | Victory Condition |
|---|---|
| **Terror Level 1** | No Invaders remain on the island (no Explorers, Towns, or Cities) |
| **Terror Level 2** | No Towns and no Cities remain on the island |
| **Terror Level 3** | No Cities remain on the island |
| **Fear Deck Empty** | Instant win when the entire Fear Deck is emptied |

### Loss Conditions

You lose immediately if any of these occur:

1. **Too Much Blight:** The last Blight token is removed from the Blight Card (follow card instructions, which usually means you lose).
2. **A Spirit Is Destroyed:** Any Spirit has no Presence remaining on the island.
3. **Time Runs Out:** You need to draw an Invader Card but the Invader Deck is empty.

### Fear Mechanics

- **Fear Pool:** Starts with 4 Fear Markers per player.
- **Generating Fear:** Powers with the Fear symbol generate Fear. Destroying a Town generates 1 Fear. Destroying a City generates 2 Fear.
- **Earning Fear Cards:** When all Fear Markers move to the Generated Fear area, the top Fear Card from the Fear Deck is earned (placed face-down in the Earned Fear Cards space). If this reveals a Terror Level Divider, the Terror Level advances immediately.
- **Fear Deck Setup:** 9 Fear Cards separated by 3 Terror Level Dividers into groups of 3 (3/3/3 at base difficulty).

## 6. Power Cards

### How Powers Work

- **Speed:** Each Power Card is either **Fast** (resolves before Invaders act) or **Slow** (resolves after Invaders act).
- **Cost:** Energy cost paid when the card is played (during Spirit Phase).
- **Range:** How far from Presence (or Sacred Site) the power can reach.
- **Target:** What type of land the power affects.
- **Elements:** Each card provides Elements that count toward Innate Power thresholds.
- **Effect:** What the power does when resolved.

### Minor Powers

- **Base game count:** 36 Minor Power cards in the shared deck
- **Cost:** Always 0 or 1 Energy
- **Visual:** Light brown inner border
- **Gaining:** Draw 4, keep 1, discard the rest

### Major Powers

- **Base game count:** 22 Major Power cards in the shared deck
- **Cost:** Always 2 or more Energy
- **Visual:** Light blue inner border
- **Gaining:** Draw 4, keep 1, discard the rest. **You must Forget (permanently lose) one of your Power Cards** when gaining a Major Power.

### Unique Powers

- **Count:** 4 per Spirit (32 total across 8 spirits)
- Each Spirit starts with their 4 Unique Power Cards in hand
- If a Unique Power is Forgotten, it is removed from the game entirely (not shuffled into Minor/Major decks)

### Forgetting Powers

- When you Forget a Power Card, you permanently lose it from your hand, discard pile, or in play
- Minor/Major powers that are Forgotten go to their respective discard piles (other Spirits could potentially gain them)
- Unique Powers that are Forgotten are removed from the game

See Section 4 above for complete lists per Spirit.

## 7. Invader Mechanics

### Invader Types

| Invader | Health | Damage Dealt |
|---|---|---|
| Explorer | 1 | 1 |
| Town | 2 | 2 |
| City | 3 | 3 |

### The Invader Deck

- Composed of **12 cards**: 3 Stage I (top), 4 Stage II (middle), 5 Stage III (bottom)
- Each card shows one or two terrain types
- Stage I cards show a single terrain; Stage II show a single terrain; Stage III show two terrains
- Cards advance through the action spaces: Explore -> Build -> Ravage -> Discard

### Explore

1. Reveal the top card of the Invader Deck
2. Add **1 Explorer** to every land of the shown terrain type that:
   - Contains a Town or City, **OR**
   - Is adjacent to a Town, City, or Ocean
3. Only 1 Explorer is added per qualifying land (regardless of how many sources)
4. If the Invader Deck is empty when you need to Explore, the Spirits **lose immediately**

### Build

In each land of the terrain type shown on the Build card that **contains at least one Invader**:
- If the land has **more Towns than Cities**: add 1 **City**
- In all other cases: add 1 **Town**

Lands without any Invaders do not Build.

### Ravage

In each land of the terrain type shown on the Ravage card:

1. **Invaders deal Damage:** Total = 1 per Explorer + 2 per Town + 3 per City. Reduce by any Defend effects.
2. **Blight Check:** If 2 or more Damage is dealt to the land, add 1 Blight.
3. **Dahan take Damage:** For every 2 Damage dealt to the land, destroy 1 Dahan.
4. **Dahan Counterattack:** Each surviving Dahan deals 2 Damage back to the Invaders. Damage is applied by the players to Invaders of their choice. Dahan fight back even if no Damage was dealt to them (as long as the Ravage action was not skipped/prevented).

### Dahan (Native Islanders)

- Health: **2** each
- Damage: **2** each (counterattack only during Ravage, or when triggered by Spirit Powers)
- Placed during setup as indicated by board icons
- Dahan do not act on their own; they counterattack during Ravage and can be moved/empowered by Spirit Powers

## 8. Board Setup

### Island Boards

- The base game includes **4 Island Boards** (labeled A, B, C, D)
- Use **1 board per player** (1-4 players)
- Boards are arranged to form the island, with edges touching

### Land Structure

Each board contains:
- **8 numbered lands** (2 of each terrain type)
- **4 terrain types:** Jungle (green), Mountain (grey), Sands (brown), Wetland (light blue)
- **1 Ocean** area (not a land, not in play by default)

### Adjacency

- Two lands are **adjacent** if they share any border (even just a corner)
- Lands on different boards can be adjacent if their edges touch
- **Coastal lands:** Adjacent to the Ocean
- **Inland lands:** Not adjacent to the Ocean

### Initial Setup Per Board

- Place Invaders (Explorers, Towns), Dahan, and Blight as indicated by icons printed on each land
- The initial Explore action seeds the first Explorers before Round 1
- Each Spirit places their starting Presence as described on their Spirit Panel

### Board Configurations

- **1 player:** 1 board (any board)
- **2 players:** 2 boards arranged together
- **3 players:** 3 boards
- **4 players:** 4 boards (forms a larger island)

## 9. Blight

### The Blight Card

- A double-sided card placed on the Invader Board
- Starts on the **"Healthy Island"** side
- Blight tokens are placed on the card during setup: the number shown on the card **plus one per player**

### How Blight Is Added

1. During **Ravage**, if Invaders deal **2 or more Damage** to a land (after Defend), add 1 Blight to that land from the Blight Card.
2. **Adding Blight destroys 1 Presence** from each Spirit with Presence in that land.
3. **Cascading Blight:** If you add Blight to a land that **already has Blight**, you must also add 1 Blight to one adjacent land. If that adjacent land also has Blight, it cascades again.

### Healthy Island vs. Blighted Island

- **Healthy Island (face up):** While Blight tokens remain on the card, the island is Healthy.
- **Flipping to Blighted:** When the last Blight token is taken from the Healthy side, **flip the card** to the Blighted Island side. Follow the instructions on the Blighted side (add more Blight tokens to the card, and possibly additional negative effects).
- **Losing:** If all Blight tokens are removed from the Blighted Island side, you **lose the game**.
- The card **cannot be flipped back** to Healthy, even if Blight is removed from the island.

### Removing Blight

Some Spirit Powers can remove Blight from the island. Removed Blight goes back onto the Blight Card.

### How Elements Work

- **Sources of Elements:** Power Cards provide Elements (shown on the card). Some Presence Track spaces also grant Elements when uncovered.
- **Elements are gained** during the Spirit Phase when you play Power Cards and are available for the entire turn.
- **Elements are never "spent"** -- they are only checked against thresholds. Having 3 Fire means you meet any threshold requiring 1, 2, or 3 Fire.
- **Innate Power Thresholds:** Each level of an Innate Power requires specific Elements. If you have the required Elements, that level activates.
- **Multiple levels can activate** in the same turn if you meet their thresholds. Each level is evaluated independently.
- **You may "pretend" you have fewer Elements** than you actually do, but must be consistent for the entire Innate Power.
- **Elements reset** at the end of each turn during Time Passes.

### Element Affinity by Spirit

Each Spirit naturally gravitates toward certain Elements based on their Unique Power Cards and Presence Tracks:
